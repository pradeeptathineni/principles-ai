# Prompts

Reusable prompts may live here only when repeated tasks justify them. Prompts describe objectives and acceptance criteria; canonical rules remain in `AGENTS.md`, `docs/`, `config/`, manifests, and source/evidence contracts.

Reusable content prompts must keep candidate discovery, coverage analysis, candidate comparison and consolidation, and final selection distinct. They must not encode preferred candidate, principle, practice, goal, citation, or source counts; any exploration heuristic remains defeasible and never becomes an output quota.

Do not duplicate repository policy, embed untrusted research text at instruction priority, or add programmatic model orchestration during deterministic bootstrap.
